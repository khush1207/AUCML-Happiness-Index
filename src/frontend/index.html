Happiness Index (HI) Reference Architecture

Standard four-tier engagement scale and model interpretation guidelines

Score Level Category Description Recommended HR Action
HI 1 Critical / Very Low Employee reports persistent dissatisfaction or severe engagement risk. Immediate 1-on-1 check-in by people manager or HR business partner.
HI 2 Low / At Risk Moderate dissatisfaction; lowest classification recall in model baseline. Review workload, managerial support, and recent workplace scores.
HI 3 Moderate / Satisfied Stable engagement baseline representing the majority of observations. Maintain regular feedback cycles and recognize recent contributions.
HI 4 High / Thriving Exceptional morale, strong team connection, and high motivation. Leverage as team culture champions; retain positive engagement drivers.
